Spitball vs Superwhisper

A simpler, free Superwhisper alternative.

TL;DR: Superwhisper is a powerful local-first dictation app with a free tier, a Pro plan ($8.49/mo) and a $249.99 lifetime license. Spitball keeps the local-first part, drops the price to zero, and keeps things simple: double-tap ⌘ and talk.

Which should you pick?

Pick Superwhisper if…

You want lots of model choices, custom prompt modes, file transcription, or Windows and iPhone.

Pick Spitball if…

You want fast, private dictation that just works with two taps, and you don't want to pay for a license.

How Spitball works

  1. Double-tap ⌘. A little island grows out of your notch and starts listening.
  2. Talk. NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes on your Mac's Neural Engine.
  3. Tap ⌘. Clean text pastes at your cursor in about 100 ms, in any app.

Questions

Is Spitball as private as Superwhisper's local mode?

Yes. Spitball only uses on-device models: NVIDIA Parakeet for speech and Apple Intelligence's on-device model for rewriting. There's no cloud option at all.

Is Spitball faster than Superwhisper?

Spitball uses Parakeet on the Neural Engine and typically returns a sentence in about 100 ms on Apple Silicon. Speed in Superwhisper depends on which model you pick.
