Spitball vs Monologue

A free, open-source Monologue alternative.

TL;DR: Monologue from Every is a lovely subscription dictation app for Mac and iPhone, around $10–15 a month. Spitball is the free, open-source take on the same idea for Mac, running entirely on your computer.

Which should you pick?

Pick Monologue if…

You want the same dictation on your iPhone with your dictionary synced, or you like Monologue's automatic vocabulary learning.

Pick Spitball if…

You want a Mac dictation app that's free, open source and private by default, with no account.

How Spitball works

  1. Double-tap ⌘. A little island grows out of your notch and starts listening.
  2. Talk. NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes on your Mac's Neural Engine.
  3. Tap ⌘. Clean text pastes at your cursor in about 100 ms, in any app.

Questions

Is there a free version of Monologue?

Monologue is subscription-based. Spitball is a free, open-source alternative for Mac with a similar double-tap-and-talk workflow.

Does Spitball have modes like Monologue?

Yes. Spitball's Styles let you pick a tone per app: as spoken, polished, formal, casual or texting, rewritten on-device by Apple Intelligence.
