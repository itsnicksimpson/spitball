Which should you pick?

Pick Wispr Flow if…

You need Windows or iPhone apps, team admin features, or you'd rather pay for a hosted service with sync across devices.

Pick Spitball if…

You're on a Mac, you want it free, and you'd rather your voice, your text and your clipboard never touch a server.

How Spitball works

  1. Double-tap ⌘. A little island grows out of your notch and starts listening.
  2. Talk. NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes on your Mac's Neural Engine.
  3. Tap ⌘. Clean text pastes at your cursor in about 100 ms, in any app.

Questions

Is Spitball really a free Wispr Flow alternative?

Yes. Spitball is MIT-licensed open source with no account and no subscription. It covers Wispr Flow's core Mac workflow: talk anywhere, Command Mode, styles per app, a personal dictionary and snippets.

Does Wispr Flow work offline?

According to Wispr's own enterprise page and independent reviews, Wispr Flow runs in the cloud and has no offline mode. Spitball runs fully offline once its speech model has downloaded.

Can I switch from Wispr Flow to Spitball?

Yes. Install Spitball, double-tap ⌘ instead of holding Fn, and add your common names and terms under Words. Your old history stays in Wispr Flow.
