How it works

Two taps. One thought.

You talk about 150 words a minute and type about 40. Spitball closes the gap in every app on your Mac: Slack, Linear, Cursor, Mail, Notes, the terminal, anywhere you can type.

01 · Tap tap
⌘command⌘command

Double-tap ⌘

A little black island grows out of your notch and starts listening. The mic warms up on your first tap, so your first word never gets clipped.

02 · Spitball

Just talk

Ramble, restart, say “um.” Your voice ripples out of the notch and hovers over your text cursor, so you always know where the words will land.

03 · Ship
⌘command→ done.

Tap ⌘ once

Clean text pastes right at your cursor, with punctuation, capitals and no “uh.” Your clipboard is put back exactly how you left it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Spitball really free?

Yes. MIT-licensed open source with no account, no trial and no paid tier. Speech recognition runs on your Mac's Neural Engine, so there's no server bill for anyone to pass on to you.

Does Spitball send my voice to the cloud?

No. Transcription runs on-device with NVIDIA Parakeet (Apple's built-in speech engine is the backup), and optional rewriting uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Your history is a file in your Library folder. The leaderboard is opt-in and only ever shares word counts.

What's the best free alternative to Wispr Flow?

Spitball. It does the same core job (talk anywhere, get clean text) with Command Mode, styles, a dictionary and snippets, but it runs entirely on your Mac and costs nothing. Read the full Spitball vs Wispr Flow comparison.

How fast is it?

About 100 milliseconds from when you stop talking to text on screen on Apple Silicon. The mic starts on your first ⌘ tap, so there's no warm-up lag either.

Does it work in Cursor, Slack, the terminal…?

If you can type in it, Spitball can type in it. It pastes at your cursor like you would, then restores your clipboard.

Does it support the Dynamic Island MacBook Pro?

Yes. Spitball draws its own island around the camera cutout on notched and Dynamic Island MacBooks, and shows a floating pill on Macs without a notch.

Why “Spitball”?

Because that's what you're doing: throwing ideas out loud and seeing what sticks. Now they stick as text.
